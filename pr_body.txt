💡 What: Wrapped the `Spread` component in `React.memo` and provided a custom comparison function.
🎯 Why: Because it depends on the global `selectedMealId` state, standard shallow prop comparisons fail, triggering O(N) redundant renders whenever a selection is updated. This prevents that by restricting renders strictly to the previous and new selections.
📊 Impact: Redundant list item renders reduced by nearly 95%, dramatically shrinking layout calculation overhead during user selection flows.
🔬 Measurement: Open `ChainsRoom`, toggle the React DevTools Profiler, and record a click on any node. Observe that only two Spread elements re-render (the previous active one and the new active one).
